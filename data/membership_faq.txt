Membership FAQ

Q: How do I become a member?
A: Complete the online membership form, pay the initiation fee, and attend the orientation session.

Q: What is the monthly membership fee?
A: Standard membership is $35 per month. Students receive a reduced rate of $18 per month.

Q: Can I bring guests to events?
A: Guests are welcome at select social events, but they must be registered in advance and follow club policies.

Q: Do I need a waiver to participate?
A: Yes. All members and guests participating in physical activities must complete a waiver before attending.

Q: How can I get event updates?
A: Event reminders are sent through the club email list, SMS announcements, and posted updates on the member board.
