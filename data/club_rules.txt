The Falcons Club emphasizes safety, respect, and inclusion for all members and guests. All members must check in at the front desk before attending practice sessions or club events. Please keep equipment clean, follow staff instructions, and report hazards immediately. Members are expected to treat teammates, coaches, and visitors with respect and maintain a positive environment both on and off the field.

Club events are open to members who are current on dues and have completed the required waiver. The club may suspend access for members who violate safety or conduct policies. Please review the latest update board for schedule changes before arriving.
